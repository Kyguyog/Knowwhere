Return to this backup?

A fresh safety backup must finish successfully before any documents are changed. This replaces documents across the entire configured database, including deleting documents added since this backup.

Stop other writers first. This operation is not atomic and may trigger Cloud Functions, extensions, or TTL policies.

Resume automatic backups?

Acknowledging recovery allows retention to remove protected backups and resumes the schedule. Check your database first.
